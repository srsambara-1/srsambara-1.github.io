About Sraavya Sambara

Hi! I'm a PhD student at MIT CSAIL and LIDS, and I'm fortunate to be supported by the NSF Graduate Research Fellowship. My research centers on efficient and reliable multimodal machine learning. I have previously worked on vision-language and language models, including improving their safety with conformal prediction, benchmarking multimodal reasoning capabilities, and investigating how language models accept false presuppositions in user queries.

Previously, I studied Computer Science at Harvard University, where I graduated magna cum laude and Phi Beta Kappa. I have worked with Professor Pranav Rajpurkar at Harvard, Professor Irene Chen at BAIR, and Professor Monica Agrawal at Duke NLP.

Beyond my academic work, I have deployed production agentic systems to Fortune 50 companies at Distyl AI, helped develop the first commercially available multi-finding abdomen-pelvis CT triage AI in the US at a2z Radiology AI, and contributed to multimodal AI research with Eric Schmidt at Schmidt Sciences.

Outside of research, I enjoy playing tennis (Harvard club tennis), running, and Carnatic music!

You can reach me at sraavyas@mit.edu. I'd love to hear from you!
